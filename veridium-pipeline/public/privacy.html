Privacy Policy

Effective October 10, 2026 · Version 2026-10-10.3

The short version

  • We collect your email, a scrambled (hashed) version of your password, and the Riot ID you link.
  • Game stats come from Riot Games' official API. They're public game data, and anyone can look up any Riot ID.
  • Your email is never shown to anyone. Your applications are visible only to you and the organization you applied to.
  • We don't sell your data, show ads, or use tracking or analytics cookies.
  • You can delete your account yourself at any time from Account settings.

This policy explains what Veridium ("Veridium", "we", "us") collects when you use this website and the services on it, why, and the choices you have. By creating an account you agree to this policy and to our Terms of Service.

1. What we collect

Information you give us

  • Account details: your email address and password. We store the password only as a one-way scrypt hash, so nobody, including us, can read it.
  • Your Riot ID (for example Name#TAG) and region, if you link one. Riot gives us a permanent account identifier (a "PUUID") for it, which we store with your account.
  • What you do on Veridium: organizations you create, roster changes and vacancies you post as an organization admin, and the vacancies you apply to, with the date and the organization's decision.
  • When you accepted these Terms and this Privacy Policy, and which version.
  • Whether you're under 18, if you tell us when applying: an optional yes/no on each application. See below.

The under-18 flag on applications

  • What we collect: when you apply to a vacancy, you can tick "I am under 18". We store only that yes/no with that one application. We never ask for your birthdate or exact age.
  • Why: only to let the organization know that, under our Terms, a parent's or guardian's consent is needed before it makes you any offer.
  • Where it's shown: only to the organization you sent that application to, on that application. It isn't shown on your profile or anywhere else on Veridium.
  • What it isn't used for: your profile, search, filtering, matching, or anything else.
  • It's self-reported and optional. We don't verify it, and ticking it or not never blocks an application. It's deleted with the application, for example when you delete your account.

Information from Riot Games

  • For any Riot ID looked up on Veridium, whether yours or one someone else searched for, we request public game data from the Riot Games API: match history, placements and results, champions and roles, ranks and league points for Teamfight Tactics and League of Legends. We cache it so profiles load quickly.
  • This is the same data Riot makes available to registered developers. It isn't tied to your Veridium account unless you link your Riot ID.

Information collected automatically

  • A session cookie that keeps you logged in (see Cookies and storage).
  • Failed login attempts per email address, kept briefly in memory to slow down password guessing.
  • Server logs kept by our hosting provider, such as IP addresses and the pages requested, which it uses to run and protect its service.

We don't use analytics, advertising or social-media tracking tools, and we don't collect payment information.

2. How we use it

  • To run your account: logging you in, keeping you logged in, and letting you link your Riot ID.
  • To build verified player profiles, the players directory, rankings and the home feed from Riot data.
  • To run recruiting: letting organizations post vacancies, letting you apply, and letting organizations review applicants and manage rosters.
  • To keep Veridium secure, prevent abuse, and enforce our Terms.
  • To contact you about your account or important changes to these policies, if we need to.

Where laws like the GDPR apply, we rely on: performing our agreement with you (running your account and the features you use), legitimate interests (security, preventing abuse, showing public game statistics), and legal obligations where they apply.

4. Who we share it with

We don't sell or rent personal information, and we don't share it for advertising. We share it only:

  • With Riot Games, to request game data: we send Riot the Riot ID or account identifier being looked up. Riot's handling of data is covered by Riot's Privacy Notice.
  • With our hosting provider, which stores our database and runs our servers on our behalf.
  • With organizations you apply to, which see your verified profile and your application, as described above.
  • When required by law, or to protect the rights, safety and security of our users or Veridium.
  • In a business transfer, such as a merger or acquisition, in which case this policy continues to apply to your information.

5. Cookies and local storage

We use one cookie, veridium_session, which is strictly necessary to keep you logged in. It holds a random token (we store only a hash of it), can't be read by page scripts, isn't sent to other websites, and expires after 30 days or when you log out. Because it's essential, we don't ask for cookie consent.

Your browser's local storage keeps a copy of your account summary (email, linked Riot ID, organizations you manage) so pages can show it straight away. It's cleared when you log out.

6. How long we keep it

  • Account information is kept until you delete your account.
  • Sessions expire after 30 days, or when you log out.
  • Applications are kept while the organization exists, unless you delete your account.
  • Cached Riot game data is public data, kept so profiles load quickly and refreshed when a profile is viewed.

7. Your choices and rights

  • See and update your information: Account settings shows your email, linked Riot ID and organizations.
  • Unlink your Riot ID from your account at any time.
  • Delete your account yourself from Account settings. This deletes your account, email, password hash, sessions, your applications, and any organization you're the only admin of. Riot game data stays, because it's public data that isn't tied to your account.
  • Ask for a copy of your information, or object to how we use it, by contacting us.

Depending on where you live (for example in the EU, UK or California), you may have rights to access, correct, delete or port your information, to object to or restrict how we use it, and to complain to your local data protection authority. We'll respond to requests within the time the law requires, and we won't treat you differently for using these rights.

8. Security

We protect your information with hashed passwords, hashed session tokens, cookies that page scripts can't read, encrypted connections (HTTPS) when deployed, and limits on repeated login attempts. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a password you don't use anywhere else.

9. Age requirement

You must be at least 16 to create a Veridium account, and we don't knowingly collect information from anyone younger. We don't verify ages, so if you believe someone under 16 has an account or has given us information, contact us and we'll delete it.

10. Where your data is stored

Our servers may be located in the United States or other countries where data protection laws differ from yours. Where required, we use appropriate safeguards for these transfers.

11. Changes to this policy

If we make material changes, we'll update the effective date and version above and ask you to review and accept the new version the next time you use Veridium.
